06 / 07 — Packaging · Artworking · Visual Systems · UI/UX

Emma
Basic

Sole designer at a premium food brand - ~5 products a day, from artworking and packaging through to digital systems, marketing and launch logistics.

01 - The problem

High volume, one designer, no drop in quality.

A premium FMCG food brand needs a constant stream of clear, retail-ready work across packaging, product, web and content - while every product drop stays coordinated across suppliers, retailers and customers, and every pack conforms to legal labelling requirements across both European and US markets.

02 - My role

Building the system while shipping the work.

I worked across concept, layout, packaging, digital design, art direction, artworking and iteration with manufacturers, content creators, chefs and production agencies across Asia.

03 - The outcome

A brand that moves quickly without losing its calm.

The system gave each product its own character while keeping the range recognisable, precise and ready to ship, supporting both existing customers and new audiences.

Many surfaces, one clear system. Designed to scale.

Designing the work, then making sure it works in the world.

Role in detail
  • Supplied product and packaging work to Selfridges, Harrods, Whole Foods and Oseyo, meeting each retailer's technical specifications and conforming to nutritional-information and legal declaration requirements across both European and US regulations.
  • Worked with content creators, chefs and manufacturers across Asia, keeping briefs, samples and product-drop logistics on track.
  • Partnered with the sales and finance teams to get products shelf-ready — reformatting photography and packaging to meet each retailer's specification, and thinking through how products would sit on shelving and in display.
  • Artworked every file solo — outlining type and vector paths and packaging print-ready files to spec — while liaising directly with manufacturers on finishes, sizing and how each substrate would perform in production.
  • Built the Amazon listings end to end: product photography, infographics and video, alongside A+ imagery and a coherent marketing-video direction with production agencies, aligned to the website I designed.
  • Used AI tools like Gemini and Claude within my process - for image generation to extend the brand's home and lifestyle photography, and to help move faster through research and iteration - producing on-brand imagery that supported the brand's direction without a full shoot.
  • Ran an in-store food demonstration at Selfridges, and designed standalone graphic advertising for marathons, fitness clubs and catalogues.
  • Helped grow Instagram views from around 5,000 to more than 500,000 while serving existing customers and attracting new audiences.
  • Presented new product ideas and designs alone to the board every week, then delivered the approved work as finished artworking.

Content direction

I designed storyboards for 25 SKUs, then briefed the production company on the animation language, pacing and feeling we were looking for. The boards gave the film team clear direction so they could execute their roles with confidence, while bringing together ideas from the wider Emma Basic team and keeping every piece consistent with the brand.

Digital system

I designed the site's wireframes and UI in Figma, then built and deployed it live on Vercel, connecting our existing domain and setting up redirects, SEO meta data and analytics along the way. I inputted data straight from Excel files to keep the nutritional information accurate and genuinely useful, built filters for gluten-free, high-protein and easy-to-snack products, and tested across devices and browsers - taking the site from first wireframe to a live, informative and easy-to-navigate product.

Product photography

Working with photographer Mark Sims, I developed the smoked salmon presentation to bring restrained hints of gold into a premium product world, while keeping the brand grounded in its tightly controlled black-and-white palette.

I carried out live product and packaging testing at events, gathering user feedback directly to inform ongoing design decisions.
