06 / 07 — Packaging · Artworking · Visual Systems · UI/UX

Emma
Basic

Sole designer at a premium, zero-additive food brand - ~5 products a day, from artworking and packaging through to digital systems, marketing and launch logistics.

01 - The problem

High volume, one designer, no drop in quality.

A premium, zero-additive food brand needs constant retail-ready output across packaging, product, web and content, coordinated across suppliers and retailers and compliant with EU and US labelling law. The brand's promise is what's not in the product, so the packaging had to earn trust through restraint, not noise.

02 - My role

Building the system while shipping the work.

I worked across concept, layout, packaging and digital design, iterating with manufacturers, chefs and production agencies across Asia.

03 - The outcome

A brand that moves quickly without losing its calm.

The system gave each product its own character while keeping the range recognisable, precise and ready to ship.

Many surfaces, one clear system. Designed to scale.

Designing the work, then making sure it works in the world.

Role in detail
  • Supplied product and packaging work to Selfridges, Harrods, Whole Foods and Oseyo, meeting each retailer's specifications and EU/US labelling law.
  • Worked with content creators, chefs and manufacturers across Asia, keeping briefs, samples and product-drop logistics on track.
  • Partnered with sales and finance to get products shelf-ready, reformatting photography and packaging for each retailer and how they'd sit in display.
  • Artworked every file solo to print-ready spec, liaising directly with manufacturers on finishes and substrate performance.
  • Built the Amazon listings end to end - photography, infographics, video and A+ imagery - aligned to the website I designed.
  • Used AI tools like Gemini and Claude to extend lifestyle photography and speed up research and iteration, producing on-brand imagery without a full shoot.
  • Ran an in-store food demonstration at Selfridges, and designed standalone graphic advertising for marathons, fitness clubs and catalogues.
  • Helped grow Instagram views from around 5,000 to more than 500,000.
  • Presented new product ideas and designs alone to the board every week, then delivered the approved work as finished artworking.

Content direction

I designed storyboards for 25 SKUs, briefing the production company on animation language, pacing and feeling. The boards kept every piece consistent with the brand while giving the film team clear direction to execute with confidence.

Digital system

I designed the site's wireframes and UI in Figma, then built and deployed it on Vercel - domain, redirects, SEO and analytics included. Nutritional data came straight from Excel, with filters for gluten-free, high-protein and easy-to-snack products, tested across devices from first wireframe to live product.

Product photography

Working with photographer Mark Sims, I brought restrained hints of gold into the smoked salmon presentation, keeping the brand grounded in its tightly controlled, uncoloured palette - a system that mirrors the zero-additive product rather than competing with it.

I ran live product and packaging testing at events, gathering feedback to inform ongoing decisions.
