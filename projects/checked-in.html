01 / 07 - Product · Editorial system

Checked
In

An AI that prints your trip as a newspaper - so you remember not just where you went, but how it felt to be there.

Product designUX / editorial systemSelf-initiated
01 - The problem

We document trips obsessively and remember them poorly.

Camera rolls fill up; the feeling evaporates by the time you're home. Checked In gives that feeling a form you can return to.

02 - My role

Iterating the product, UX and editorial system.

I moved from flows and prototypes into repeated interface iterations, refining the conversation, the issue structure and the visual rules that hold every page together.

03 - The outcome

A keepsake that earns its place.

A working concept for personal, AI-generated newspapers: useful as a product, but memorable as an object.

The camera roll keeps the evidence. Checked In keeps the feeling.

Context / booklet front pageBooklet front page for an exhibition: a printed guide that explains why Checked In exists, how the app works, and how a personal memory becomes a newspaper issue.
