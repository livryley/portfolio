01 / 07 — Product · Editorial system

Checked
In

An AI that prints your trip as a newspaper - so you remember not just where you went, but how it felt to be there.

Product designUX / editorial systemSolo · 3 weeks
01 - The problem

We document trips obsessively and remember them poorly.

The brief was open: design an AI product that assists people in daily life, any medium. The constraint was real - it had to exist as a tangible piece for exhibition, not a screen demo, in three weeks. Camera rolls fill up and the feeling evaporates by the time you're home, so the outcome had to give that feeling a form you could actually hold.

02 - My role

Iterating the product, UX and editorial system.

Solo project, start to finish. I moved from flows and prototypes into repeated interface iterations, refining the conversation, the issue structure and the visual rules that hold every page together.

03 - The outcome

A keepsake that earns its place.

A working concept for personal, AI-generated newspapers, built to be picked up and read at the exhibition - useful as a product, but memorable as an object.

The camera roll keeps the evidence. Checked In keeps the feeling.

Context / booklet front page

Booklet front page for an exhibition: a printed guide that explains why Checked In exists, how the app works, and how a personal memory becomes a newspaper issue.

Feel first. Write later.

Start each day by checking in with your mood. Your emotional state helps shape how your story is told, because memory isn't just about what happened, but how it felt. This helps us aid your good days and bad days.

Meet your Travel Buddy

A thoughtful AI companion that chats with you throughout your trip, asking questions, storing memories, and capturing how the moment felt. Later, it uses everything you've shared to help write your personalised travel newspaper, so you can feel all of those moments again.

04 / 06The design is yours - from fonts and colours to layout and mood, every detail is customisable, so your newspaper looks just as personal as the memories inside it.

Don't forget what it felt like

Once your newspaper is ready, you can print it, share it digitally, or keep it just for you. Want to tweak something after it's done? You can - every edition stays editable until it feels just right for you. The story isn't finished until you say it is, because you decide how your story is told.
