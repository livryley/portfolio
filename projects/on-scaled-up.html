ON:
Scaled Up

Deciding how to market a shoe on the promise that you'll stop noticing it's there.

Art direction · videographyBranding · UI/UXTeam of 2 · 5 weeks
01 — Problem

The product's best quality is invisible.

Brief: tell the story of On's Swiss-engineered apparel technology to a Gen Z audience already hooked on activewear, built around the line "Feel Nothing. To Feel Everything." The challenge was selling a sensation, not a feature list - a platform-first idea that could travel from social to a real-world activation.

02 — Role

Concept and UX.

In a team of two over five weeks, I led art direction, videography, branding and UI/UX - designing a digital journey and interactive quiz around feeling rather than spec.

03 — Outcome

A campaign you move through.

An interaction-led concept entered into D&AD.

The product's best quality is that it feels invisible. So make the experience weightless.

Find your fit

A personality quiz suggests a shoe based on your hobbies and interests, whether that's clubbing or hiking - a small, personal moment built into a big brand.
