I'm Olivia — a graphic designer who builds the systems behind a brand: packaging on a shelf, an app people open every day, an identity that gives a feeling somewhere to live. Previously the sole designer at a FMCG premium food company, running the whole process and talking to manufacturers more than to other designers.

In a room

I don't really believe in "throwaway" work. A two-inch sticker gets the exact same energy from me as a billboard over a motorway. I'm just genuinely interested in making things that feel honest, slightly unexpected, and considered down to the bone.
