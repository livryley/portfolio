Info — about Liv

I'm Liv — a graphic designer who builds systems for things you can only feel. Digital product, identity, editorial and artwork; currently the sole designer at a premium food brand, running the whole process and talking to manufacturers more than to other designers.

In a room

I don't really believe in "throwaway" work. A two-inch sticker gets the exact same energy from me as a billboard over a motorway. I'm just genuinely interested in making things that feel honest, slightly unexpected, and considered down to the bone.
